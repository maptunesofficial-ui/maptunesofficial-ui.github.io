# MapTunes Support

MapTunes gives turn-by-turn driving directions and can sing them over the music you are already playing.

## Contact

**Open a request:** [github.com/maptunesofficial-ui/maptunesofficial-ui.github.io/issues](https://github.com/maptunesofficial-ui/maptunesofficial-ui.github.io/issues/new)
(a free GitHub account is needed). A support email address will be added here before launch.
Please never include passwords or payment details in a request.

## Common questions

**Why are some directions spoken instead of sung?**
A direction is sung only when it can be sung clearly and correctly in time. When the connection is weak, the song is unknown, the turn is
close, or a word might be misheard, the direction is spoken instead. That is deliberate: a clear spoken direction is safer than an unclear
sung one.

**How do I turn sung directions off?**
Settings → Sung directions.

**How do I delete my data?**
Settings → Delete my data. It removes what MapTunes keeps on your phone and asks our server to forget your install. See the
[Privacy Policy](../privacy/).

**How do I stop sharing my arrival time?**
Tap **Stop sharing** while the trip is running. A shared link also ends by itself when the trip ends.

**How do I cancel my subscription or the free trial?**
On your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → MapTunes. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid renewal.
Refunds are handled by Apple: [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

**MapTunes cannot find my song.**
Allow Apple Music access, or allow the microphone so Apple's ShazamKit can recognise the song. MapTunes never records or uploads audio.

**A direction was wrong.**
Always follow road signs and the law first. Please tell us where and when (date, time, the direction you heard), without personal details.
