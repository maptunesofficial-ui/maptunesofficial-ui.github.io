## 2. Driving safely

- Keep your eyes on the road. Road signs, signals, road markings, the police and the law always come before any direction from MapTunes.
- Directions, distances, street names and your location can be wrong, late or missing. If a direction does not match the road, do not

- Do not handle your phone while driving. Set your destination before you set off, or let a passenger do it.
- Obey the law where you drive about the use of phones and navigation devices.

## 3. What MapTunes does

MapTunes gives turn-by-turn directions. When a song is playing on your phone and an internet connection is available, a direction may be
**sung** over your music; otherwise, or when you switch sung directions off in Settings, it is **spoken**. Your music keeps playing in your
music app; MapTunes does not play, record or upload your music.

## 4. Services MapTunes relies on

Maps, routes and place search come from Apple Maps and from Stadia Maps (built on OpenStreetMap data). Song information comes from FreqBlog
through our server. Sung directions are made on our servers. These services can be slow or unavailable; when they are, directions are
spoken or may not be given. We do not promise that the service will be uninterrupted or error-free.
